# Demo credits

Every piece of art, animation, sound and music in the demo is dedicated to the public domain
under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Some of it was made with
AI tools; each such item says so below, and they are gathered under
[AI-generated content](#ai-generated-content).

## Made for Open MMORPG (CC0)

The following were created by Brenden Vaughan for this demo and are dedicated to the
public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/):

- the bow, staff, arrow, quiver and short sword under `Art/Weapons` and their icons, generated
  with [Ludo AI](https://ludo.ai) on a paid plan, then retopologised, re-baked and finished in
  Blender (the original `.glb` files and the scripts are in `Art/Weapons/Source~`);
- the Viking shield (`Art/Weapons/VikingShield.fbx`), generated with
  [Ludo AI](https://ludo.ai) on a paid plan;
- the horse (`Meshes/Horse.fbx`, `Textures/Horse_*`), the deer (`Meshes/Deer.fbx`,
  `Textures/Deer_*`), the border collie (`Meshes/Collie.fbx`, `Textures/Collie_*`) and the
  wolf (`Meshes/Wolf.fbx`, `Textures/Wolf_*`; the wolf pup is the same mesh, scaled down),
  whose skeletons and clips are the Quaternius animal rigs listed below - the wolf wears the
  collie's. The four meshes were generated with [Tripo](https://www.tripo3d.ai) on a paid
  Pro plan, which gives the maker full rights to the output;
- the Wayside Shrine (`Art/Shrine`), generated with [Ludo AI](https://ludo.ai) on a paid
  plan;
- the horse's saddle (`Art/Saddle`), generated with [Ludo AI](https://ludo.ai) on a paid plan,
  then fitted to the horse and its rider, retopologised and re-baked in Blender (the original
  `.glb` and the scripts are in `Art/Saddle/Source~`);
- the icons under `Textures/Icons/Items`, `Textures/Icons/Skills`,
  `Textures/Icons/Attributes` and `Textures/Icons/Badges`, generated with
  [Ludo AI](https://ludo.ai) on a paid plan;
- the menu background, loading screen and title (`Textures/OpenMMORPG-menu-background.jpg`,
  `Textures/OpenMMORPG-loading.jpg`, `Textures/OpenMMORPG-title.png`), generated with
  Google Gemini;
- the bow animations (`Animations/Bow_Draw`, `Bow_Release`), the four `Island_*` ground
  textures (generated from the Quaternius noise sheet), the armour and shield icons
  under `Textures/Icons/Armor` (renders of the Quaternius outfits and the Malagen shield),
  the guild crests, the terrain under `Terrain`, the interface icons `Textures/circle.png`,
  `next.png` and `shield.png` (drawn in code), and everything else the demo's editor tools
  generate.

## Sound effects (all CC0 1.0)

Every sound effect under `Audio` is released under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). CC0 asks for no attribution,
but these were edited from recordings on [Freesound](https://freesound.org), and their makers
are credited with thanks:

- `AimedShot1` — [FX bow&arrow](https://freesound.org/s/511490/) by Lydmakeren
- `ArrowFire1` — [FX_bow&arrow2.wav](https://freesound.org/s/511489/) by Lydmakeren
- `ArrowImpact1`–`ArrowImpact3` — [arrow_.hits_target.wav](https://freesound.org/s/521242/) by cyoung510
- `WaterSplash1`–`WaterSplash3` — [Splashing Water.wav](https://freesound.org/s/241846/) by rafael45

All other sound effects under `Audio` were generated with AI models and are released under
CC0 1.0.

The demo finds sound effects by file name (see the README), so any of them can be swapped for
another CC0 clip of the same name without touching code.

## Music (CC0 1.0)

The four tracks under `Audio` — `Theme du Chevalier`, `Ruined Temple`, `Cavernous Droning` and
`The Unresolved Path` — were generated by Brenden Vaughan with [Suno](https://suno.com) on a
Pro plan, under which Suno assigns its rights in the output to the maker
([Suno's Terms of Service](https://suno.com/terms)). They are dedicated to the public domain
under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The files carry the
song ids and "made with Suno" tags that Suno embeds.

## AI-generated content

- **Music:** all four tracks, made with Suno.
- **Sound effects:** every clip under `Audio` except the Freesound edits listed above, made with
  AI audio models.
- **3D models:** the horse, deer, border collie and wolf (Tripo); the bow, staff, arrow,
  quiver, short sword, Viking shield, the Wayside Shrine and the horse's saddle (Ludo AI).
- **Images:** the item, skill, attribute and badge icons (Ludo AI); the menu background,
  loading screen and title (Google Gemini).
